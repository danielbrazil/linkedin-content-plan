# Beyond the interview answer: a full-stack learning series

Date: October 30, 2026
Status: Draft - awaiting review; not published

## LinkedIn post

Knowing the definition is one thing. Explaining when to use it, why it matters, and what can go wrong is another.

That is the idea behind a learning series I am starting here on LinkedIn.

Over the next eight weeks, I will share two posts each week about the technologies I work with: C# and .NET, JavaScript and TypeScript, React, Angular, SQL Server, PostgreSQL, and Azure.

The starting point is a collection of 200 interview questions, organized into related topics. The goal is not to memorize 200 answers. It is to connect interview preparation with everyday engineering decisions.

Each week will have two parts:

- Tuesday: related interview questions, focusing on the reasoning and trade-offs behind the answers.
- Thursday: a closer look at the concepts, with practical examples.

For example:

- Not just "What does async/await do?" but "When does Task.Run help, and when is it the wrong tool?"
- Not just "What is dependency injection?" but "What happens when service lifetimes do not match?"
- Not just "What is an index?" but "Why is this query still slow?"

I want to make this useful for developers preparing for interviews and for anyone revisiting concepts they use every day.

The first pair of posts arrives next week, starting with C# and .NET fundamentals.
